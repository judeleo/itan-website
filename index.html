About ITAN

Every work holds a story. We give it a room.

ITAN is an art centre built for creative art in all its forms — painting, photography, design, fashion and the gatherings that bring them to life.

We're opening our doors with a three-day celebration: a place to look slowly, shop the work of makers you love, and stay late with the people who make the culture.
